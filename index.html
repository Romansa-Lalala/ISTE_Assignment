Welcome to Our Malware Tutorial

Malware is malicious software designed to damage systems, steal information, disrupt devices, or gain unauthorized access to data. Because computers and mobile devices are used every day, understanding common types of malware is an important part of using technology safely. So as long as technology and social media is present in your life, you should know at least this basic information to not get in trouble.

What You Will Learn

This tutorial introduces several common forms of malware and explains how they work. The first lesson focuses on viruses and worms. The second introduces Trojans, spyware and ransomware. The final lesson explains how malware can spread between devices and describes basic ways users can reduce the risk of infection. All of the lessons should teach you how to avoid and detect malicious software and links.

The tutorial is designed for beginners, so no previous cybersecurity knowledge is required. There will be some technical terms that are explained in the tutorial for better understanding.
