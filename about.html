About Our Logo

The logo shows a laptop with a "CONFIDENTIAL" folder being pulled out by a hook. The hook represents phishing, the bug represents malware, and the folder represents the private data attackers try to steal. It visually sums up the site's topic: hidden online threats trying to steal your data.
